# Video Prompt

Output hook, timeline, narration, shot list, visual prompts, transitions, subtitle keywords, and cover prompt.

The hook may create curiosity but must not strengthen factual claims beyond their grounding. Prefer demonstrations and concrete examples. Do not include an automatic publishing step.
