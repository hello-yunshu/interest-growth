# Generic Writing

Write for clarity and traceability.

- Use the selected grounding references instead of inventing facts.
- Keep uncertainty close to the statement it constrains.
- Distinguish observation, opinion, interpretation, and factual claim.
- Preserve links to notes, sources, practice artifacts, projects, or reviewed claims for audit.
- Do not make the prose sound more certain merely to improve engagement.
- Keep publication behind explicit human approval.
