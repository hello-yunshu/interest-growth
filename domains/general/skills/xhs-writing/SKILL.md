# XHS Interest Writing

Produce 3–5 title candidates, a concise body, tag suggestions, card outline, grounding notes, and risk review.

Avoid fabricated authority, universal promises, unsupported certainty, or claims that a result has already been published. Keep a clear distinction between personal learning record, practical experience, and externally verifiable fact.
