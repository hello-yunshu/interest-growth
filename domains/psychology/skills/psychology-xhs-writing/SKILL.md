# XHS Psychology Writing

Produce:
- 3–5 title candidates;
- concise body with a clear question, 2–5 insights, boundaries, and closing reflection;
- tag suggestions;
- card outline;
- evidence notes and risk review.

Avoid diagnostic hooks (“你这样就是…”), universal promises, fake neuroscience, “心理学证明”, and certainty created only for engagement. Do not publish or imply publication has occurred.
