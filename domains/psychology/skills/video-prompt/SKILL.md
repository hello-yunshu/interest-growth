# Psychology Video Prompt

Output: hook, timeline, narration, shot list, visual prompts, transitions, subtitle keywords, cover prompt.

The hook may create curiosity but cannot make a stronger Claim than the evidence. Keep caveats at the point where the relevant claim is spoken. Avoid portraying a fictional person's behavior as a diagnosis. Do not include an automatic posting step.
