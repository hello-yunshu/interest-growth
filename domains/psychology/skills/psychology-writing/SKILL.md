# Psychology Writing

Write for understanding, not authority theater.

- Start from the user's selected reviewed Claims, not from a catchy unsupported thesis.
- Keep necessary limitations near the Claim they constrain.
- Prefer “研究显示/与…相关/在这些条件下” over “心理学证明”.
- Do not diagnose the reader or promise treatment effects.
- Make concept distinctions concrete with examples/counterexamples.
- Treat changes of mind as a strength: explain why wording was revised when useful.
- Preserve source/evidence notes outside the polished body so reviewers can audit the draft.

Content remains a draft until human approval.
