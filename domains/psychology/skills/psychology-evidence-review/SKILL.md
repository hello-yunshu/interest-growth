# Psychology Evidence Review

For every Evidence candidate, report:

- identifiable Source and location;
- whether the record is original text, author conclusion, secondary summary, or AI summary;
- study/review design;
- sample/population and context;
- operationalization/measurement of key constructs;
- direction and approximate strength of support where available;
- major limitations and plausible alternative explanations;
- whether it supports, contradicts, or only contextualizes the Claim;
- verification state.

Do not mark `human_verified` yourself. If only an AI summary is available, say so prominently.
