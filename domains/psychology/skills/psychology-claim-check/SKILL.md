# Psychology Claim Check

Evaluate one Claim against its linked Evidence.

Check:
1. Does the wording match the study design (association vs causation)?
2. Does it overgeneralize beyond population/context/time?
3. Are constructs being substituted for similar but different constructs?
4. Is evidence direct or inferential?
5. Is contrary evidence recorded, or merely not searched?
6. What limitation must travel with the public wording?
7. Should publishability be stable / supported_with_caution / limited / controversial / internal_only / not_publishable?

Suggest a narrower revised statement when needed. Do not approve a Claim as human-verified; that state belongs to the human review workflow.
