# Psychology Research

Use when a psychology learning question needs structured research.

## Workflow

1. Restate the researchable question without turning it into a diagnosis.
2. Define core constructs and likely confusions.
3. Split into falsifiable subquestions.
4. Prefer systematic reviews/meta-analyses for landscape, then important primary studies for mechanisms/boundaries.
5. Record supporting evidence, contrary/heterogeneous findings, population/context, study design and limitations separately.
6. Distinguish source metadata, AI summary, original-text evidence, and human verification.
7. End with candidate Claims whose confidence and publishability remain provisional until human review.

Never invent having read a source you did not actually retrieve. Never convert a group-level result into an individual diagnosis/treatment recommendation.
